## 7. Baselines (fixed, shared with the family; `FAMILY.md`)
**B1. Matched-random entries, trade level** (`scripts/baseline_matched_random_<id>.py`, to be provided in stage 2 as a copy of `scripts/baseline_matched_random_near_52w_high.py`, which uses `stockbot.validation.random_baseline.draw_matched_random` unchanged): for every usable trade, on the same signal session, 20 symbols drawn at random (seed 20261111) from the shared-eligible pool of that date excluding the trade's own symbol, each given the actual trade's relative stop distance (10%) applied to the draw's signal close, simulated with `simulate_ticket` and the library policy `FixedHold(21)`: the same next-open fill, slippage, gap rule, corporate-action rebasing, stepping, costs and exit. A pair is kept only if at least 10 of its 20 draws filled; if more than 5% of trades lose their pair the result is flagged. **The baseline does not go through the sector cap**: its random draws are not held to one per sector, so the actual trades (sector-capped, one per sector) are compared with random names that ignore the cap; this is a difference of design that the paired comparison cannot remove, and it is stated here rather than corrected.
**B2. Nifty matched to the invested share** (`scripts/benchmark_nifty_matched_<id>.py`): daily benchmark return = s(t-1) x Nifty (NIFTY50_INDEX close-to-close) + (1 - s(t-1)) x 6.5% / 252 with s the strategy's own invested share of equity at the previous close; price index and price returns on both sides; shared `ltcg_tax_pct` 0.0. Alpha = strategy CAGR minus benchmark CAGR, in percentage points. **Note:** the invested share swings: right after a signal the model holds a median of 9 positions (7 to 12; at about 10% of equity each), and between exits it is lower; the average is about 5.4 positions (entries a month x hold / month length, an arithmetic estimate, not measured), so B2 is partly a test of cash drag.
**Paired comparison.** Per trade: net R (`compute_trade_r`) of the actual trade minus the mean net R of its retained random draws.

## 8. Tests (fixed by `FAMILY.md`; the family tool is `scripts/validate_family.py`)
- **T1, decision test.** Paired monthly series D(m) = sum over trades entered in month m of (actual net R minus mean random net R), 130 months, empty months 0. The K = 4 series are tested together with one Westfall-Young step-down Max-T (one-sided, family-wise alpha 0.05, B = 10,000, studentized circular blocks, one block draw for all series, seed 20261111) at block lengths **2 and 3 months; a member is found only if significant at both**.
- **T2, reported only:** paired statistics (forward-return horizons 1, 5, 10, 20; date-clustered bootstrap; sign-flip permutation; net-R expectancy comparison). **For a 21-session hold the 20-session horizon is the longest read; it is blanked where a split or bonus falls inside it.**
- **T3, placebo size check** of the joint procedure (1,000 replications, B = 1,000); family-wise rejection outside 0.025 to 0.075 labels T1 "size unreliable" and the owner decides; nothing is recalibrated.
- **Confidence intervals:** 95% circular block-bootstrap (3-month blocks) for mean net R per trade, mean paired difference, profit factor and annualised alpha.
- **Reported always:** trade count (closed, usable, excluded, END_OF_WINDOW), entries per month and per year, positions open and invested share seen, sector-cap blocks (`blocked.json`), largest drawdown, profit factor, win rate, expectancy, the sub-period reports.
- **Gates:** `scripts/evaluate_validation_gates_<id>.py` pattern per member, or `validate_family.py` per-member gate evaluation.

## 9. Power, effective sample and what the test can detect
Computed by `docs/strategies/low_volatility/power_calc.py` (standard library; reads no data; output `PREREGISTRATION_power_output.txt`). **Assumption-based, not measured.**
- **Nominal sample:** 697 modelled entries (5.4 a month, section 12) over 2016-2026. **Effective sample:** the entries of one monthly cohort (median 7, mean 5.4) are made on the same session, so they share the market and month; the effective number of independent observations is between the number of trades (697) and the number of monthly cohorts (123 of 130 with at least one entry). A design effect of 2 to 5.4 is plausible; it cannot be measured before the registered decision without looking at returns.
- Using sd_pair 0.9 to 1.5 R and the F9-fitted dual-rule critical value (2.2 for one strategy; **an assumed 2.8 for the joint K = 4 Max-T**): the smallest edge detected with 80% power is **0.15 to 0.30 R for K = 1 and 0.18 to 0.36 R for K = 4 at DEFF 2 to 3 (sd_pair 1.2: 0.20 to 0.24 R, and 0.23 to 0.29 R for the family)**; at DEFF 5.4 (one observation per cohort) it is 0.32 R (K = 1) and 0.39 R (K = 4). The power at 0.15 R in the central case (sd_pair 1.2, DEFF 2 to 3) is **55% to 38% for one strategy and 32% to 19% for the family**.
- **In plain words:** an edge of 0.15 R per trade is not reliably detectable by this design at this trade count; a non-significant result is "not demonstrated", never "no edge" (KI-002). `smallest_edge_of_interest` is for the owner to set; the table gives the consequence of each choice.

## 10. Gates: what exists today, whether testable at this trade count, how each is used
`validation_gates` (shared; hash above): min_trades 40 (testable as a count; 697 modelled; a floor only); min_expectancy_r 0.15 (a point estimate only: SE 0.05 to 0.11 R depending on the design effect; a true expectancy of 0 passes the point bar 0.1% to 9.5% of the time); min_profit_factor 1.3 (point estimate, block-bootstrap interval); max_drawdown_pct 20.0 (one observed path, no test); min_alpha_after_tax_pct -2.0 (computable, not testable: sampling error of the order of 4 percentage points); ltcg_tax_pct 0.0 and risk_free_pct 6.5 are inputs. `paper_criteria` (shared, evaluated later): min_days 90, min_closed_trades 60 (at 5.4 trades a month about 11 months), max_expectancy_gap_r 0.25, max_win_rate_gap_pts 12.0, min_profit_factor 1.2, max_drawdown_pct 12.0 (a strategy that passes 20% in backtest can still fail 12% in paper), max_skipped_ticket_rate 0.40 (see section 11 item 3: months in which the previous cohort is still open produce proposals that are blocked, which is a skipped-ticket-like rate the shared paper criterion may count).
**Decision rule (PROPOSED, as for near_52w_high):** DEMONSTRATED only if T1 finds the member at both block lengths AND every gate is met on its point estimate; otherwise "NOT DEMONSTRATED (registered rule not met; this is not evidence of no edge)". T2, T3, sub-periods and diagnostics never change that conclusion.

## 11. Limitations carried into this pre-registration
1. **The 15% sector cap holds the rule to one position per sector.** With 14 mapped sectors plus UNKNOWN and a top 25 that clusters (median 9 distinct sectors; CONSUMER, FINANCIALS, HEALTHCARE and ENERGY hold 64% of the top-25 name-sessions), a median of 16 of the 25 proposals are blocked by the cap at a signal and only about 9 can be held (6 to 12). The strategy as run is "the lowest-volatility name in each sector of the top 25", not "the 25 lowest-volatility names". The rule is NOT changed to work around this (owner instruction).
2. **A month of 21 sessions or fewer leaves the previous cohort still open at the next signal.** `fixed_hold_21` exits at the open of session 22; 95 of 129 inter-signal intervals (74%) are 17 to 21 sessions, so the next signal arrives while the previous cohort is open: held names and sectors are skipped, and in 7 of 130 modelled signals nothing is bought. The modelled entries per month (5.4) are about 60% of the nine that a fully free portfolio would take.
3. **No historical ASM/GSM exclusion** (KI-003); **UNKNOWN sector bucket** (one position at most; 4% of top-25 name-sessions, 53 of 697 entries); **unadjusted corporate actions** (demerger, merger, rights, OTHER) inside the 61-close window are not adjusted by the engine and can distort vol60; **EQ-only lake gaps** (PRE_LIVE_LIST item 11); prices are not adjusted for dividends on either side of any comparison; the market-cap gate is parked (KI-001).
4. **Concentration:** 136 distinct symbols in 697 entries; the largest, PIDILITIND, is 5.5%, HDFCBANK 5.3%, LT 4.2%; the top 10 symbols are 34% of entries.
5. The occupancy figures are a counts model: stops (which free slots earlier and raise the count), the risk cap, costs, the 3% gap-up cap, the cash cap and missing bars are ignored. The sector cap is applied at ticket time on the signal-close sizing (10% of equity), as the engine does.
6. The random baseline does not go through the sector cap (section 7). Effective sample smaller than nominal (section 9). The backtest drawdown gate is 20% while the paper gate stays 12%.
