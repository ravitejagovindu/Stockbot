## 2. The rule, in exact words
The rule is riskadj_momentum's code, copied verbatim into `stockbot/strategies/momentum_calm/strategy.py` (a strategy may not import another strategy; `is_signal_session`, `assess`, `initial_stop` and `order_signals` are identical copies, and a unit test proves that with the filter off the picks equal riskadj_momentum's). Definitions, as in that code:
- **Signal sessions:** only the first trading session of each calendar month (its (year, month) differs from the previous calendar session's). The 252-session calendar window must exist, so the first possible signal is 2016-12-01 (119 signal months; the 11 earlier ones are empty, as before).
- **History guards:** a bar on the signal session; at least 240 adjusted closes inside the last 252 calendar sessions ending at it; 253 adjusted bars of the symbol's own (t-21 and t-252 are positions on its own bars); finite positive closes.
- **Momentum:** r12 = adjusted close[t-21] / adjusted close[t-252] - 1, required **> 0 strictly**. vol = sample sd (ddof 1) of the daily log returns of the consecutive adjusted closes t-252 through t-21, required > 0. **Score s = r12 / vol.** Order: s descending, exact ties by higher raw 20-session ADTV, then symbol. The top 25 are proposed with ordinal rank 25 down to 1.
- **Stop and exit:** initial stop = signal close - max(3 x ATR14, 0.08 x signal close), ATR14 the Wilder ATR on the adjusted high, low, close of the symbol's last 252 bars; a stop at or below zero makes the name unusable. Exit `mc_trailing_atr3_floor8`: reference = highest close since entry (seeded with the entry price); each session new stop = max(live stop, reference - max(3 x ATR14 of that session, 0.08 x reference)); it never moves down; set on bar T it applies from bar T+1; no time exit. Re-entry: no rule beyond riskadj_momentum's (it has none; the engine's own rules apply).
- **THE ONE NEW ELEMENT, the calm filter.** On each signal date, **sigma252** = sample standard deviation (ddof 1) of the **252 daily log returns of the symbol's own consecutive adjusted closes ending at the signal date t** (the last 253 own bars, sessions t-251 through t; the same volatility as lowvol_uptrend's). It is computed for **every eligible name that passes the history guards** (the names that have a sigma252), **before** the r12 > 0 filter. A name without 252 returns has no sigma252 and is never proposed. **median252 = the median of sigma252 over ALL of those names** (an even count: the mean of the two middle values). A name is **calm if sigma252 <= median252** (a tie at the median is calm). Only calm names may be proposed: among them keep r12 > 0 (and every other condition above), rank by s, propose the top 25 (fewer if fewer qualify; a month is never padded with non-calm names). One reading to state: "the 252 sessions t-251..t" is taken as the 252 returns of the closes t-252..t, which are the 253 bars the rule already requires.
- **Universe:** shared eligibility, no overrides.

## 3. Why
- Momentum's drawdown came from jumpy leaders: the highest-scoring names tend to be the ones with large daily swings, and the registered momentum runs missed the 20% drawdown gate (monthly 34.3% at 1.0% risk in family_batch2 and 23.7% at half risk in family_batch6, stored results).
- The low-volatility literature finds that low-risk stocks earn returns comparable to or better than high-risk ones, so restricting momentum to the calm half is a natural single change.
- Low volatility alone (lowvol_uptrend, family_batch3) came close on expectancy (0.14 against the 0.15 gate, stored result).
- The question: **does the registered momentum rule, restricted to the calmest half of the eligible stocks, find T1 significant and meet all five gates?** The filter is the single change; nothing is tuned.

## 5. The baseline pool rule (stated plainly; open question for the owner)
**B1 uses riskadj_momentum's pool rule unchanged:** for each actual trade the 20 random symbols are drawn on the same signal session from the **shared-eligible pool of that date** (never the trade's own symbol, no other pool filter), **not restricted to calm stocks**. What this means: the calm filter is part of the strategy's selection, so **any benefit of simply owning calm stocks counts as the strategy's edge** in T1 (the trade's R minus the mean R of its random draws), not as something the baseline already has. A T1 pass would therefore not show that momentum works inside the calm half; it could come from the calm half alone. Whether the random pool should instead be restricted to calm stocks (which would isolate momentum within calm names and is the stricter test of the momentum part) is **an open question for the owner. It is not decided here: this text registers the unchanged pool**, and changing it later is a new trial.

## 6. The honest note
- This is **the tenth look at the same ten years** of data; no other family is counted in K = 1, so K does not carry the earlier trials. **A pass would be weak evidence.**
- **The calm filter may leave the stocks nearly the same as plain momentum.** The overlap check (counts only, 119 signal months, `OVERLAP.md`): the mean overlap of the 25 picks with plain riskadj_momentum's 25 is **0.358** (median 0.32, min 0.04, max 0.84), so on average about 9 of 25 picks are shared. The calm half held a median of 96 calm names with r12 > 0 per month (min 23, max 255); in 1 month fewer than 25 calm candidates exist. The picks differ, but the overlap says nothing about how their returns differ.
- **No tuning.** The median cut-off is fixed now; any other cut (a third, a quartile), window or volatility measure is a new trial. The overlap threshold 0.85 that decided whether this text would be written was fixed before the number was seen.
- With K = 1 the Max-T step-down is the single test.

## 9. Limitations (additional to the earlier texts, which apply in full)
The BE/BZ sell model is an assumption. sigma252 looks backward from the signal date and a calm name can turn jumpy. The median is taken over the shared-eligible names that have 252 returns, a thin set in the first signal months (about 140 to 200 names in 2017). A flat series has sigma 0 and counts in the median. The filter changes which names fill the 12 slots and the sector spread, so the trade count and the invested share differ from plain momentum in a way not forecast here (the trade rate 1.9 is the plain rule's slot arithmetic). No dividends, no historical ASM/GSM, incomplete sector map and EQ-only buying, as in the earlier texts.
