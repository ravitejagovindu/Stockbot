## 3. Why, and the question asked
The drawdown gate was the one that failed in the earlier runs: the monthly rule 34.3% (family_batch2), the any-day rule 36.1% (family_batch4) and **45.6%** (family_batch5, with BE/BZ data), against the registered limit of 20%. The question: **can either rule meet all five gates, the 20% drawdown gate included, at half the risk per trade?** It is one question about sizing, asked of two members.

What half size does mechanically (a description, not a forecast): a position's value is risk divided by the stop distance (about 0.5% of equity / stop distance), so with the same stops each position is about **half the size** and the book is about half as exposed; with the 12-position limit and the 15% caps the account holds much more **idle cash**, which is credited at 6.5% in the alpha figure (so alpha changes through the cash credit as well as through the stocks); fewer of the 12 slots or of the 15% risk budget are binding, so some entries blocked earlier by cash or caps can now happen; **the capital path differs, so the run is NOT a rescale of the earlier trades** (different entries, different cooldown and slot timing, different equity at each entry). The sizing does not change which stocks the rule picks.

## 4. The honest note
