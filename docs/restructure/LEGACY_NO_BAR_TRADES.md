**Could the old backtests have traded names while they were in a restricted segment?** For classes A and B: they "traded" on days when no trade was possible — a flaw of the pre-swap data, now removed by the swap. For class C (48 entries, 2.3% of 2,129) and E (4): very likely some were in a non-EQ series or a surveillance stage, because the old Pass 1 ran **without any ASM/GSM history** (none exists, KI-003) and the lake contained bars for days outside the EQ series. I cannot prove the series. The effect is small (90 of 2,129 entries, 4.2%) and the six configurations are already rejected; it does not change any decision, but it is one more reason that the old win/loss numbers must not be used as evidence for a new strategy.

Method notes: classification rules in order — not a Nifty session today → A (flat) ; flat bar with |move| ≥ 4.5% → B; symbol's first date in today's lake after the entry date → D; absent for ≥ 5 consecutive Nifty sessions from that date → C; otherwise E. Nothing was written to any database.
